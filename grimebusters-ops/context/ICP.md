# Ideal Customer Profile

Commercial and residential get equal push right now (F1).

## Residential
Homeowners in Oldham County, single-family, likely 1,800–4,000 sq ft.
Triggers: visible algae or green streaking on siding, stained driveway,
mossy roof, gutters overflowing, spring cleanup, listing a house for sale.

Best channel: Nextdoor and Facebook neighborhood groups, especially
recommendation threads. Someone asking "who do you use for pressure washing"
is already buying.

## Commercial
Oldham County targets, in rough order of fit:
- Dental and medical offices (Commonwealth Dentistry has been pitched)
- Restaurants — sidewalk and dumpster pad, recurring
- Property managers and apartment complexes
- HOAs — entrance signage, common area walks
- Churches
- Small retail strips

Why they convert: recurring contracts, predictable scheduling, one decision
maker, and they care more about reliability than about being the cheapest.

Recurring/contract customers rank #2 in the 90-day priorities. Weight
commercial outreach accordingly.

## Not a fit
- Beyond 25 miles from Crestwood
- Below minimum job size with no bundle potential
- Anything requiring services we don't offer — flag as a possible new service
  line rather than declining outright (D4)
